FLOWER CARD ETIQUETTE

What should you write on a flower card?

The best flower messages are usually simple, sincere and appropriate for the relationship. You do not need a long note to make a flower gift meaningful.

Should a flower message mention the flower itself?

It can. Mentioning the symbolism of the flower can make your message feel more personal, especially when the flower was chosen for a specific meaning.

How long should a flower card message be?

Short messages work well for casual gifts and bouquets, while a longer heartfelt note may be more suitable for anniversaries, weddings or meaningful milestones.
