FLOWERS & ASTROLOGY

What is a zodiac flower?

Zodiac flowers are symbolic flower associations connected with the traditional personality themes of each zodiac sign. Different sources may use different flowers, so these pairings are best understood as creative and cultural associations rather than fixed scientific rules.

Why do zodiac signs have flower associations?

Flowers have long been used to represent emotions, virtues and personality qualities. Over time, botanical symbolism and astrology have been combined to create flower associations for the zodiac signs.

Can my zodiac sign have more than one flower?

Yes. There is no single universal list. Different traditions and modern interpretations may connect a sign with several flowers. LeoFlora presents one fitting symbolic match for each sign.
