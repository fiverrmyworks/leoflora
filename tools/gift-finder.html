How to choose flowers with meaning

A thoughtful flower gift is about more than choosing the prettiest bloom. Occasion, relationship, color and symbolism can all change how a bouquet feels.

Should I choose flowers by meaning or appearance?

Ideally, both. A flower can be visually beautiful while also carrying a meaningful sentiment that fits the moment.

Do flower colors change the message?

Yes. Different colors can suggest affection, admiration, friendship, celebration or calmness. Context matters, so LeoFlora combines color, personality and occasion when creating recommendations.
