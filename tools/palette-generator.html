How to use botanical color palettes

Botanical palettes take inspiration from flowers, leaves, soil, natural light and seasonal landscapes. They can work especially well for branding, websites, invitations, interiors and visual content.

How many colors should a palette contain?

Five colors provide a useful balance: one or two dominant colors, supporting tones and a contrasting accent. You do not need to use every color equally.

Can I use these HEX colors commercially?

Color values themselves are not exclusive to LeoFlora. You can use the displayed HEX colors as inspiration for personal or commercial design work.
