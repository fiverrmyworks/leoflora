THE LANGUAGE OF FLOWERS

What is a birth flower?

Birth flowers are flowers traditionally associated with each month of the year. Like birthstones, they are often used to represent personal qualities, seasonal themes and meaningful sentiments.

Different traditions can sometimes associate more than one flower with a month. LeoFlora presents commonly recognized birth flowers while explaining their symbolism in an easy-to-explore format.

Can a month have more than one birth flower?

Yes. Botanical traditions have developed across different places and periods, so some months are associated with multiple flowers. This finder focuses on one widely recognized flower for each month.

How can I use my birth flower?

Birth flowers can inspire birthday gifts, bouquets, artwork, wallpapers, wedding details, personal color palettes and meaningful messages for someone special.
