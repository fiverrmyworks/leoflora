CREATE & PERSONALIZE

Make botanical wallpapers for your favorite screens.

The LeoFlora Wallpaper Studio creates each design directly in your browser. Your custom text and settings are rendered using HTML5 Canvas, so no account or paid design software is required.

What sizes can I create?

You can create wallpapers for phones, desktop screens, social stories, square posts and Pinterest-style vertical graphics.

Does LeoFlora upload my design?

No. The wallpaper is generated locally in your browser using the options you choose.

Can I download the wallpaper?

Yes. Press Download PNG and your browser will create a high-resolution PNG version of your current design.
