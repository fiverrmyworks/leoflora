Chrysanthemum meaning & symbolism

Chrysanthemums are associated with joy, friendship, longevity and positive wishes in many traditions.

🌼

What does Chrysanthemum symbolize?

Chrysanthemums have rich cultural significance and can symbolize happiness, loyalty, longevity and respect. Their meaning varies widely across regions and colors.

Flower symbolism can vary by culture, historical period and color. LeoFlora presents common modern and traditional associations while recognizing that meanings are not always universal.

Chrysanthemum meaning by color

Red Chrysanthemum

Affection and heartfelt admiration

Yellow Chrysanthemum

Friendship and cheerful energy

White Chrysanthemum

Pink Chrysanthemum

Gentle affection and appreciation

Chrysanthemum meaning in love

Red and pink chrysanthemums can communicate affection and admiration, while their fuller symbolism often emphasizes lasting connection.

Chrysanthemum meaning in friendship

Yellow and lighter chrysanthemums are especially suitable for friendship, appreciation and cheerful celebrations.

When should you give Chrysanthemum?

Birthdays, friendship gifts, congratulations, appreciation, seasonal arrangements and family celebrations.
