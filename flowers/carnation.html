Carnation meaning & symbolism

Carnations are traditionally associated with affection, admiration, devotion and lasting appreciation.

🌸

What does Carnation symbolize?

Carnations are valued for their long-lasting blooms and wide range of colors. Their symbolism often centers on affection, loyalty and appreciation, although individual colors can communicate different sentiments.

Flower symbolism is influenced by culture, history, color and context. For that reason, the same flower can carry slightly different meanings depending on how and where it is used.

Carnation meaning by color

Red Carnation

Affection and admiration

Pink Carnation

Gratitude and gentle love

White Carnation

Sincerity and pure intentions

Yellow Carnation

Cheerfulness and warm friendship

Carnation meaning in love

Red and pink carnations can express affection and admiration, making them suitable for romantic or deeply personal gifts.

Carnation meaning in friendship

Pink, yellow and lighter carnations can work well for friendship, gratitude and cheerful celebrations.

When should you give Carnation?

Birthdays, Mother's Day, appreciation gifts, congratulations, anniversaries and everyday bouquets.
