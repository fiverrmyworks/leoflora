FLOWER SYMBOLISM

Why do flowers have meanings?

Flowers have been used to communicate emotions, celebrate milestones and represent ideas across many cultures and historical periods. Meanings are not always universal, and the same flower may carry different associations depending on color, place and context.

Does flower color change its meaning?

Often, yes. For example, red roses are strongly associated with romantic love, while yellow roses are more commonly associated with friendship and warmth.

Are flower meanings universal?

No. Flower symbolism can differ across cultures, traditions and historical periods. LeoFlora presents commonly recognized meanings while noting that context can influence interpretation.
