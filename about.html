About LeoFlora

LeoFlora.art is an independent botanical discovery website focused on flower meanings, symbolism, gifting, color inspiration and creative interactive tools.

What we do

We publish easy-to-explore flower guides and browser-based tools including birth flower discovery, zodiac flower associations, gift recommendations, message ideas, botanical color palettes, personality quizzes and wallpapers.

Our approach

Flower symbolism can vary between cultures, traditions and historical periods. LeoFlora presents commonly recognized interpretations while explaining that meanings are not always universal.

Our goal

Our goal is to make botanical symbolism useful, beautiful and enjoyable to explore while creating original resources that help visitors choose, understand and celebrate flowers.
