ABOUT LEOFLORA

Flowers have stories. We help you discover them.

LeoFlora.art is a botanical discovery website built around flower meanings, symbolism, gifting, color inspiration and interactive creative tools.

What is LeoFlora.art?

LeoFlora.art is an independent digital project designed to make botanical symbolism easier and more enjoyable to explore. The site combines educational flower guides with interactive tools such as birth flower discovery, zodiac flower associations, gift suggestions, message generation, botanical palettes and personalized wallpapers.

Our goal is to create a useful and visually engaging resource for people interested in flowers, meaningful gifts, design inspiration and botanical culture.

Our approach

Flower symbolism varies between cultures, historical periods and traditions. LeoFlora therefore presents commonly recognized interpretations rather than claiming that every meaning is universal.

We aim to distinguish traditional symbolism, creative interpretations and entertainment-focused tools clearly so visitors can understand the context behind the information they are reading.
